Support

Put the phone in the middle of the table, add the players in seating order and press Start. The host says a starting word and who goes first. Take turns saying a word linked to the last one before the timer runs out. Repeat a word, run out of time or say something unrelated and you're out. Last player standing wins.

Common questions

The game doesn't hear anyone
  • Check that Link Up has microphone and speech recognition access: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and → Speech Recognition.
  • Put the phone face up in the middle of the table, away from music or a TV.
  • Speak your word clearly; you don't need to shout. The ring around the HALT button shows when it's listening.
The judge says it's unavailable

The smart judge uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Without it, the game still works: every word is accepted, and your group settles disputes with HALT.

The judge got a word wrong

Press HALT. To reverse an accepted word, tap it to put that player out. To bring back a player whose word was rejected, choose Bring back. Link Up saves your decision as a house rule and follows it next time the same words come up. You can see your house rules, or forget them all, in Settings.

Can we play in another language?

Yes. In Settings → Language you can play in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese or Hindi, or mix two languages in one chain. Your iPhone needs speech recognition for that language.

Turns are too fast or too slow

Change the turn time (3–20 seconds) and the speed-up in Settings → Timer, or pick the Party preset for longer turns.

Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Link Up is a party game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. We don't collect, sell or share any personal information.

What the app uses, and why

  • Microphone and speech recognition. During a turn, Link Up listens for the player's word and turns it into text with Apple's speech recognition, which is built into iOS. Depending on your iPhone and language, Apple may process this audio on the device or on Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy. Link Up never records, saves or sends audio anywhere itself.
  • Judging links. Words are judged by Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone.
  • Saved on your phone. Player names, settings, custom presets and house rules are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also forget house rules in Settings.
  • Photos. Link Up never accesses your photos. The permission description is only listed because a library inside the app includes photo support.

Children

Link Up doesn't collect information from anyone, including children. The Family preset turns on the profanity filter and kid-safe starting words.
